INSTRUCTIONS: HOW TO CONNECT YOUR METATRADER 5 ACCOUNT TO THE DASHBOARD

Step 1: Get your Personal API Key from the Dashboard
--------------------------------------------------
1. Open your browser and navigate to https://mt5connector.uk
2. Sign in to your account (or click "Sign Up" to create a new free account).
3. In the top right corner of the dashboard, copy your unique "API Key" string.

Step 2: Authorize the Dashboard URL in MT5 Options
--------------------------------------------------
By default, MetaTrader 5 blocks outbound internet connections. You must whitelist the server domain:
1. Open your MetaTrader 5 terminal.
2. Go to Tools -> Options (or press Ctrl+O).
3. Click on the Expert Advisors tab.
4. Check the box "Allow WebRequest for listed URL:".
5. Double-click "<add new URL...>" and type:
   https://mt5connector.uk
6. Click OK.

Step 3: Run the Expert Advisor (EA)
-----------------------------------
1. Press F4 in MT5 (or go to Tools -> MetaQuotes Language Editor).
2. Open AccountStatsConnector.mq5 located in your workspace:
   mql5\AccountStatsConnector.mq5
3. Click "Compile" (or press F7). Ensure compiler shows "0 errors, 0 warnings".
4. Go back to MetaTrader 5. In the Navigator panel (Ctrl+N), expand Expert Advisors.
5. Drag and drop "AccountStatsConnector" onto any chart.
6. In the inputs dialog:
   - InpApiKey: Paste your personal API Key from Step 1.
   - InpServerURL: Set to https://mt5connector.uk/api/sync
   - InpSyncIntervalMinutes: 30 (or your preferred auto-sync frequency).
7. Click OK and ensure "Algo Trading" is toggled ON (green icon) in MT5 top toolbar.

Step 4: Verify the Sync
-----------------------
1. Open the Experts log tab in MT5 (Ctrl+T -> Experts tab).
2. You will see: "AccountStatsConnector: Sync succeeded. Server response: {"success":true,...}"
3. Refresh your dashboard at https://mt5connector.uk.
4. Your account balance, trades, win rates, and EA performance metrics will appear live!
